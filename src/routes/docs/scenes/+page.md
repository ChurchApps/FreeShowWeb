# Scenes

> Create Scenes to simplify the control of your global environment across multiple displays. This offers a simple method to change all output streams without affecting the software operator's view. 

Scenes can be found in the "Functions" tab in the drawer. They can be triggered with a click, triggered when a slide is activated or activated by an action.

A Scene is a saved blueprint for your screens. It tells FreeShow exactly which layers to show or hide (like backgrounds, text, or timers) and how to style them on each individual monitor or live stream feed.

## Examples:

A Scene acts like a filter for each display. With one click, you can change:
	* Screen 1 (Main Projector): Show all the layers. Give them the full experience.
	* Screen 2 (Live Stream): Hide the background video layer (make it transparent) and shrink the text down to use a lower-thirds style.
	* Screen 3 (Booth Monitor): Only show the plain text on a black background.

## Using Scenes

The simplest method to use a scene is to drag the scene from the Functions drawer onto a slide in your show. This will cause the show to change to that slide when the operator gets to that slide. If an instant change is required, such as the minister coming on stage during the song worship, simply double-clicking the scene in the drawer will activate it. 

## Category Scenes

Another more permanent method to set scenes is via the Category method. 

You can right click a show category to add a certain action to that, that will trigger each time a show in that certain category is triggered. A scene action needs to be created for each type of category that you wish to activate a scene. The action will simply be to start a specific scene. 


## FAQ

- **What is the difference between an Output Style and a Scene?**

Output Styles are permanent layout rules for a screen (like always forcing lower-thirds on the stream). Scenes are dynamic switches that let you change those rules on the fly mid-service with a single click.

- **Why should you use Scenes instead of just changing slides?**

They allow you to radically change what your audience and live stream see without messing up the software operator's preview screen. You can completely wipe out background videos on your stream or change text sizes in the room instantly.

- **Can one Scene control multiple screens at the same time?**

Yes. A single Scene acts like a master switch. When activated, it instantly reconfigures, for example, your main projector, your live stream feed, your stage confidence monitors, and your foyer TVs all at once.
